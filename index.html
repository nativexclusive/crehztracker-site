FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is CrehzTracker for?

People who work for themselves: musicians and artists, freelancers, consultants and small business owners. Pick a profile — Musician & Artist, Consulting and Business, or Personal — and keep separate books per profile or one shared set.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free at launch, and there is nothing to cancel.

Do I have to link my bank?

No. CrehzTracker never asks for a bank login. Entries come from what you scan, forward, tap, say, write or type.

How does tap to pay work?

A purchase made with Apple Pay is logged by a Shortcuts automation you set up once on your own phone — it takes about two minutes, and the steps are in Settings › Money › Tap to pay. Apple does not let apps ship a personal automation, so this is the one thing you set up by hand. Some card issuers never trigger it; those purchases can be scanned or dictated instead.

What happens to my data?

Your books are stored on our servers so they survive a lost phone and reach your other devices. Receipt text is sent to Anthropic to be read, and the assistant’s questions go there with your ledger — only after you allow AI data sharing. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps. Everything is in the privacy policy.

Can I delete everything?

Yes, from inside the app: Settings › Account › Delete account removes your account, every entry and every receipt image from our servers, and clears the phone.

Is this tax advice?

No. CrehzTracker gives estimates for planning. Confirm figures with a tax professional before filing.

Does it work offline? On Android?

It works offline and catches up when it is back online. It is an iPhone app; there is no Android version.

Something else? Visit support or email info@crehztracker.com.
